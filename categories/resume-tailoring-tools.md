# Resume Tailoring Tools

Resume tailoring tools help you adjust your resume for a specific job. Tailoring is not lying — it's choosing the most relevant projects, skills, and bullet points for each role.

Tailored resumes consistently get more callbacks than one generic resume sent everywhere.

## Resume tailoring checklist

Before applying:

- Is the JD title (or a close equivalent) reflected in your resume?
- Are the top 5-7 required skills present?
- Are your strongest related projects near the top?
- Did you remove unrelated clutter?
- Are all claims honest?
- Did you re-check formatting after editing?

## Anti-patterns

- Adding skills you cannot defend in an interview
- Tailoring so aggressively that bullets become generic
- Removing your strongest unique work to fit a JD
- Sending the same "tailored" resume to 50 roles
