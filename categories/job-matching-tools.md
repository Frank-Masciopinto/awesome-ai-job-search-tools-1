# Job Matching Tools

Job matching tools help candidates find jobs that fit their resume, skills, location, and goals — and ideally explain *why* a role does or doesn't fit.

A good match should help you decide:

- Which jobs are worth applying to
- Why a role fits (skills, seniority, location)
- What's missing
- What resume changes may help

## Good job-matching signals

A good match should explain:

- Skill overlap
- Missing skills
- Location and work-authorization fit
- Seniority fit
- Resume keyword gaps
- Why the role may be worth applying to (or skipping)

## Bad signals

- "98% match" with no explanation
- Same roles surfaced as "top match" for very different resumes
- Match score that goes up just because you applied to similar roles before
- No way to remove obviously wrong matches
