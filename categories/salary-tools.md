# Salary Tools

Salary data is your leverage in negotiation. Know your number before any offer call.

## Tools

| Tool | Website | Best for | Pricing | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Levels.fyi | https://www.levels.fyi | Tech compensation by level | Free | Strongest tech comp data (base + equity + bonus) |
| TryApplyNow Salary Estimator | https://www.tryapplynow.com/tools/salary-estimator | Quick salary estimates | Free | Role + location-based estimate |
| Glassdoor | https://www.glassdoor.com | Reviews + salaries | Free | Broad data; varies in quality |
| Payscale | https://www.payscale.com | Salary surveys | Free | Strong for non-tech roles |
| Salary.com | https://www.salary.com | Salary calculators | Free | Useful for general roles |
| H1B Salary Database | https://h1bdata.info | LCA-disclosed salaries | Free | Public H-1B filings |
| Comparably | https://www.comparably.com | Comp + culture data | Free | Reviews + salaries |
| LinkedIn Salary | https://www.linkedin.com/salary/ | Salary insights | Limited free | Built into LinkedIn |
| Blind | https://www.teamblind.com | Anonymous tech discussion | Free | Comp threads (verify before trusting) |

## How to use salary data well

1. Pick 3 sources, not 1. (Levels.fyi + LinkedIn + Glassdoor is a good combo.)
2. Filter by **company, level, location, and year** — old data is stale.
3. Separate **base, equity, bonus**. The total is what matters but you negotiate each piece differently.
4. Know your **walk-away number** before the call.
5. Ask for the **range** of the role before naming your number.

## Negotiation cheats

- The first offer is almost never the best offer.
- Recruiters expect you to negotiate.
- "I have competing offers" only works if it's true and you can name them.
- Equity vesting matters: 4 years, 1-year cliff is standard; ask about it.
