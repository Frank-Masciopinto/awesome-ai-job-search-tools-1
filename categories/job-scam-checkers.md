# Job Scam Checkers

Job scams are surging — fake recruiters, fake offers, fake remote AI/data gigs, fake check-deposit schemes. A scam checker is anything that helps you verify a posting before you reply.

## Strong scam signals

- Recruiter only contacts via Telegram, WhatsApp, or Signal.
- Company email uses Gmail, Outlook, or a free domain.
- You're "hired" with no real interview.
- They ask you to **buy your own equipment** and "we'll reimburse you."
- They send a check for "more than needed" and ask you to wire the difference back.
- They want SSN, bank account, or ID **before any signed offer**.
- Salary is wildly above market.
- The JD is copy-pasted from a real company but the contact isn't.
- Apply link goes to a Google Form / random Notion page instead of the company's careers page.
- "AI training" or "data labeling" gig asking for an upfront fee.

## Yellow flags

- Vague role title with no real tech stack.
- Brand-new company website with no real content.
- Company LinkedIn page with <50 employees but claims huge funding.
- Recruiter LinkedIn has no posts, no history, stock-photo profile.
- "Urgent" hiring pressure to skip your checks.

## Steps before responding

1. Search `"<Company name>" scam` and `"<Company name>" fake job`.
2. Visit the company's careers page directly — find the role listed.
3. Check the recruiter on LinkedIn — tenure, mutual connections, post history.
4. Confirm the email domain matches the company's real domain.
5. Never send ID documents until you have a signed offer letter.

## If you already replied

- Stop the conversation immediately.
- Don't click new links.
- Block the contact.
- Report the post to the platform (LinkedIn, Indeed, etc.).
- If you shared sensitive info, report identity fraud locally (US: [IdentityTheft.gov](https://www.identitytheft.gov)).
