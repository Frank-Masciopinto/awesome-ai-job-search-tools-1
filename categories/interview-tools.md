## What to practice

For software engineering:

- Data structures + algorithms (arrays, hashmaps, trees, graphs, DP)
- System design (for new grad: scoped; for experienced: deeper)
- Behavioral (STAR format: Situation, Task, Action, Result)

For data science / ML:

- SQL (joins, window functions, aggregations)
- Statistics + A/B testing
- ML fundamentals + case studies
- Product sense (for product DS roles)

For data analyst:

- SQL deeply
- Excel
- BI dashboarding
- Stakeholder scenarios

## Behavioral prep — STAR templates

For each likely question (conflict, failure, leadership, ambiguity), have 2 stories:

- **Situation:** what was the context
- **Task:** what was your responsibility
- **Action:** what you specifically did
- **Result:** measurable outcome

Practice out loud, not just in your head.
