# Chrome Extensions for Job Seekers

Chrome extensions can save real time when applying — autofill, one-click resume drop, saving job posts to a tracker — but they also touch sensitive data (resume, email, sometimes payment info), so pick carefully.

## Tools

| Tool | Website | Best for | Pricing | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Simplify | https://simplify.jobs | Autofill + tracker | Free | Autofills applications across major ATS systems |
| Teal | https://www.tealhq.com | Save jobs from any page | Freemium | Pairs with the Teal tracker |
| Huntr | https://huntr.co | Save jobs + track | Freemium | Pairs with the Huntr board |
| LinkedIn Sales Navigator (limited) | https://www.linkedin.com/sales/ | LinkedIn lookups | Paid | Not free, but useful for outreach |
| Hunter.io | https://hunter.io | Email lookup from any page | Freemium | Right-click email finder |
| RocketReach | https://rocketreach.co | Contact lookup | Freemium | LinkedIn-integrated lookups |
| ChatGPT for Google / Sider / Monica | (various) | Resume / cover letter drafting in-page | Freemium | Choose one carefully — wide permissions |
| Grammarly | https://www.grammarly.com | Writing assistant | Freemium | Reads what you type — review their privacy policy |

## What to check before installing any extension

- Read the **permissions** prompt (does it really need "read and change all data on all websites"?).
- Read the **privacy policy** — what data do they store?
- Check the **publisher** — is it the real company?
- Check the **review count** and recency.
- Avoid extensions that ask for your resume *and* your bank info.
- If you don't use it within a week, uninstall it.

## After your job search

When you finish a search, **uninstall the extensions you don't need anymore**. They keep collecting data as long as they're installed.
