# Email Finder & Referral Tools

Referrals raise the odds of an interview by 3-10x at most companies. Email finders help you reach the right person — a teammate, a hiring manager, or an alum at the company.

Useful for:

- Warm outreach before applying
- Referral requests
- Follow-ups after applying
- Recruiter contact
- Learning more about a team

## Safe outreach rules

Do:

- Be honest about who you are and why you're writing
- Keep messages short (under 120 words)
- Reference the specific role
- Ask for advice or a referral politely
- Respect "no" or no response

Do not:

- Spam 20 people at the same company
- Pretend you already know someone
- Send misleading claims about your background
- Demand a referral
- Re-message someone who said no

## A simple outreach playbook

1. Find the role you want to apply to.
2. Identify 1-2 people on the team via LinkedIn.
3. Find their work email with an email finder.
4. Send a short, personalized note (one ask, not five).
5. Follow up once after 5-7 business days.
6. Thank them either way.
