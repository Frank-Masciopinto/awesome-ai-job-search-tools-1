# How to Choose an AI Job Search Tool

There are hundreds of tools. The best tool depends entirely on your current bottleneck. Find the bottleneck first, then pick the tool.

## Step 1 — Diagnose your bottleneck

In the last 4 weeks:

- Applied to **fewer than 5** roles → bottleneck is **finding good roles**.
- Applied to 20+ but got **no replies** → bottleneck is **resume / ATS**.
- Got screens but no onsites → bottleneck is **interview prep**.
- Got onsites but no offers → bottleneck is **late-round signal** (deep dive prep, communication).
- Got offers but feel underpaid → bottleneck is **negotiation / salary data**.

## Step 2 — Match bottleneck to tool type

| Bottleneck | Tool type | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Finding good roles | [Job matching tool](../categories/job-matching-tools.md) | TryApplyNow, Jobright, LinkedIn |
| Resume gets no replies | [ATS resume checker](../categories/ats-resume-checkers.md) | TryApplyNow ATS Score Checker, Jobscan |
| Tailoring takes hours | [Resume tailoring tool](../categories/resume-tailoring-tools.md) | TryApplyNow, Teal, Jobscan |
| Losing track of apps | [Job tracker](../categories/job-trackers.md) | Teal, Huntr, Simplify |
| No referrals | [Email finder / referral tool](../categories/email-finder-referral-tools.md) | TryApplyNow Email Finder, Hunter |
| Suspicious postings | [Job scam checker](../categories/job-scam-checkers.md) | BBB, FTC, Job Fit Checker |
| Interview anxiety | [Interview tool](../categories/interview-tools.md) | Pramp, Interviewing.io, Big Interview |
| Underpaid | [Salary tool](../categories/salary-tools.md) | Levels.fyi, Glassdoor |

## Step 3 — Try one tool at a time

Don't sign up for 8 tools in one weekend. You won't use them.

- Pick one tool for your top bottleneck.
- Use it for 2 weeks.
- Measure: did your reply rate, interview rate, or close rate change?
- Keep or drop based on real data.

## Step 4 — Know what to avoid

Avoid any tool that:

- Promises **guaranteed** interviews or jobs.
- Applies to roles **without showing you what it submits**.
- **Fills in screening answers** (visa, salary, work auth) **without your input**.
- Hides what it's doing behind a paywall before you can audit it.
- Uses fake reviews or fabricated success stories.
- Makes cancelling hard.

- [Email Finder](https://www.tryapplynow.com/tools/email-finder)
- [Salary Estimator](https://www.tryapplynow.com/tools/salary-estimator)

Spend money only when a free tool is clearly the bottleneck.
